08 / BEHIND THE SCENES

PRODUCTION JOURNAL

ENTRY 01 // METAHUMAN MOCAP SOLVE

Successfully processed source video footage into Unreal Engine to animate the digital MetaHuman facial rig. We opted for a markerless workflow to retain the subtle physical nuances and micro-expressions of the actor without the heavy cleanup of optical dots.

ENTRY 02 // PIPELINE AUTOMATION

Developed custom Python scripts and PySide6 interfaces within Maya to accelerate the asset handoff. Geometry layout from Blender is now cleanly exporting via USD into our UE lighting environments, ensuring non-destructive iterations.

ENTRY 03 // BATTLEFIELD TOP-DOWN DYNAMICS

Executed the final top-down interactive wipe system. We transitioned the proxy layout directly into the greyscale blocking phase to dial in camera tracking for the RPG impact, utilizing Houdini ragdoll simulations for the background robot wreckage before final texture application.

ENTRY 04 // FINAL RENDER DEADLINE

Locked the look development for Aditya Gokhale's cinematic close-ups and Unit-07's rusted chassis. Compositing workflows are currently underway in Nuke. Target completion deadline: March 31, 2027.

EXT. ADITYA'S LAB - EXPERIMENT AREA


Aditya explains the purpose of the clandestine experiment and assures everyone that the procedure is completely safe. As part of the experiment, everyone is given a drink called "NIRA".


Agastya accepts the glass but hesitates. He smells the drink before taking a small sip. A strong pungent odor catches his attention. One by one, the participants collapse onto the beds. Their bodies go limp.


His vision begins to blur. The laboratory begins to stretch and distort around him. His strength leaves him instantly.


MONTAGE: Bright surgical lights illuminate the room. His body is surgically opened from the center... Neural cables connect the biological brain to artificial processors. Mechanical limbs replace damaged human tissue.
